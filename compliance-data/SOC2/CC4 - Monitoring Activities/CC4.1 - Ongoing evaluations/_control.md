# CC4.1 - Ongoing evaluations

- **Framework:** SOC 2 2017 TSC (rev. 2022)
- **Category:** CC4 - Monitoring Activities
- **Control ID:** CC4.1
- **Owner:** _unassigned_
- **Review cadence:** annual
- **Next review:** _set in app_

## Objective
The entity performs ongoing and separate evaluations of its controls.

- [ ] Approved policy in `policies/`
