# CC8.1 - Change management

- **Framework:** SOC 2 2017 TSC (rev. 2022)
- **Category:** CC8 - Change Management
- **Control ID:** CC8.1
- **Owner:** _unassigned_
- **Review cadence:** annual
- **Next review:** _set in app_

## Objective
Changes to infrastructure, data, software and procedures are authorised, tested and approved.

## Evidence checklist
- [ ] Approved policy in `policies/`
- [ ] Documented procedure in `procedures/`
- [ ] Current evidence in `evidence/`
- [ ] Completed forms/records in `forms/`
